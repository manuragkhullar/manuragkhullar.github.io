About me

I’m a PhD student in the School of Computing and Information at Pitt, advised by Zheyuan Ryan Shi.

I study natural language processing, multilingual evaluation, and reliable LLM agents for healthcare. My work asks how language and phrasing affect model decisions, and how to make those decisions more reliable.

At Penn, I studied transformer interpretability with Li Shen. I was also a Data Science for Social Good Fellow at UW Seattle.

My education also spans the liberal arts through the Young India Fellowship at Ashoka University. Before graduate school, I worked on science and technology policy at NITI Aayog, the Government of India’s apex policy think tank.
